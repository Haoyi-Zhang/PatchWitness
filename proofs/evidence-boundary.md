# Evidence boundary

The artifact makes no security label predictions. The owned mathematical inputs
have no CVE, repository, patch label, timestamp or correspondence to any third-party
vulnerability. No source programs, vulnerable binaries, exploits or live services
are executed. Abstract index comparisons are specification operations, not loads.

An accepted certificate is a positive existential claim about two expected IR
expressions at the same assignment. It neither establishes that all old faults
were removed nor that no regression was introduced. Real security relevance and
prospective prioritization require independent public labels and measured ranking
outcomes. A zero real-case count is not zero precision or zero coverage.

The proposed hypotheses are untested: neither accepted nor rejected. A failure to
acquire a usable cohort in this session is not a proof that no lawful corpus can
be assembled. The current prototype also lacks a C/C++ lowering bridge. Merely
reacquiring CSV files would not turn it into a validated source-analysis system.

The recent SPatch paper already analyzes fine-grained patches through differential
symbolic execution; established witness-validation and proof-carrying-code work
already separates generation from checking. This prototype has not established a
novelty delta beyond those ideas adequate for the intended FSE research question.
